# SOP: Lithium Battery Shipments

All shipments containing lithium batteries (Class 9 dangerous goods) require:
- IATA DGD for air, IMO declaration for sea
- Surcharge: EUR 80 sea LCL, EUR 150 air per shipment
- Customer must provide MSDS before booking confirmed

Standalone batteries (UN3480) and batteries-in-equipment (UN3481) have different handling.
Confirm classification with customer before quoting.
