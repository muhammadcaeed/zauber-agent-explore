# SOP: Fuel and Bunker Surcharges

Sea freight quotes must include current BAF (Bunker Adjustment Factor):
- Asia-Europe trade: EUR 180/TEU as of Q4 2025
- For LCL: prorated by weight, minimum EUR 25 per shipment

Air freight fuel surcharge is set monthly by IATA index.
Road freight: included in line haul rate, no separate surcharge.

If quote is requested with validity > 14 days, add a fuel-fluctuation clause.
